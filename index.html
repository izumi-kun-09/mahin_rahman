Research

Academic work

My research sits at the intersection of marketing, behavioral economics, and consumer psychology.

Under Review 2025

Controversy as a Marketing Event

Analyzing the Impact of the Niemann–Carlsen Scandal on Global Chess Search Interest

A quantitative study using Google Trends data to examine how the cheating scandal between Hans Niemann and Magnus Carlsen drove measurable spikes in global chess search interest. Analysis includes t-tests, Mann-Whitney U, Cohen's d effect sizes, and Pearson correlations across six results tables.

BBA Thesis 2024–25

Nostalgia-Driven AIDA Progression Theory

A conceptual and quantitative framework examining how nostalgia mediates consumer behavior across AIDA stages in Bangladesh's vintage and second-hand book market. Methods include SEM via SmartPLS, eight hypotheses, and the Southampton Nostalgia Scale.
